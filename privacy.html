Privacy notice

This notice covers the owner-operated Private Mail Integration using Hermes Agent (Google OAuth project name: Hermes AI Drive). It is for the account owner's use, not public signup.

Access and purpose

With the account owner's authorization, Gmail read-only access permits retrieval of message metadata, bodies, and attachments for requested internal tasks such as reviewing forwarded correspondence, bookkeeping, and inventory reconciliation. It does not permit sending, deleting, or changing email. The integration does not request Google Drive access.

Processing and storage

Selected email content may be sent to OpenAI through the service configured in Hermes for requested analysis. Outputs and supporting evidence may be retained in local working files, session and chat history, logs, and business records. Processing is not confined to the operator's computer. Provider handling depends on the applicable service terms and account settings.

No fixed retention period or automatic deletion is promised. This public site contains only explanatory information; mailbox contents and credentials are not published here. The operator does not sell Gmail data or use it for advertising.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the Google authorization in Google Account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete previously saved files, chat records, backups, or provider-held copies. Retained copies require separate handling.

Questions and deletion requests

Contact the operator through this project's GitHub issues. Issues are public: do not include email contents, credentials, or other private information. Ask for a private follow-up if needed. Deletion requests are subject to any applicable recordkeeping obligations and provider controls.
