Private Mail Integration

This is an owner-operated, personal email integration using Hermes Agent. Its Google OAuth project is named Hermes AI Drive. It is not offered for public signup.

The integration requests Gmail read-only permission to locate and read selected messages and attachments for the account owner's internal administration, bookkeeping, and inventory reconciliation. This permission does not authorize sending, deleting, or modifying mail. No Google Drive access is requested for this integration.

Selected email content may be processed by OpenAI through the service configured in Hermes. Working files and conversation records may be retained locally. This website hosts only information about the integration, not the mailbox or its credentials.
